# Yukiguni Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

SEVEN DOT, LIMITED LIABILITY COMPANY ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the iOS application "Yukiguni" ("the App") handles.

## 1. Information We Collect

**We do not collect any information that personally identifies you, such as your name, email address, or location.** The App has no account registration or login.

Your game progress is stored only on your device and is never sent to our servers or to any third party (see Section 2, "Information Stored On Your Device"). However, to improve the App, we collect anonymous usage data that is not linked to you personally (see Section 5, "Sharing With Third Parties," for details).

The App includes the RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) SDK to manage in-app purchases; only purchase transaction information and an anonymous app user ID randomly generated by the SDK are sent to RevenueCat. The App also includes the PostHog (PostHog, Inc., EU region) SDK to understand how the App is used (see Section 5, "Sharing With Third Parties," for details). In addition, the App includes the Meta Platforms, Inc. SDK to measure the effectiveness of the ads we run (see Section 4, "Advertising and Tracking," for details).

## 2. Information Stored On Your Device

The App stores the following information only inside your iPhone / iPad (in the App's own storage area). None of this data is sent to our servers or to any third party.

- **Game progress**: the state of your slopes, courses, lifts, and facilities, your coins, construction progress, unlocked maps, and similar data.
- **Purchased items (cache)**: whether you own the permanent items is also stored on your device, so their effects stay active even when you launch the App offline.

Because game progress exists only on your device, the App has no feature to transfer your progress to another device.

## 3. Access to Device Features

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, photos, contacts, or any other device feature or personal data.

However, the App asks once, through App Tracking Transparency (ATT), for permission to use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) (see Section 4, "Advertising and Tracking," for details).

## 4. Advertising and Tracking

The App shows no ads.

The App bundles the Meta Platforms, Inc. ("Meta") SDK to measure the effectiveness of, and optimize the delivery of, the ads for the App that we run on Meta (Facebook and Instagram). The following information is sent to Meta:

- **App events**: events recorded automatically by the SDK, such as launching the App.
- **Device information**: basic information such as your device model, OS version, and app version.
- **Advertising Identifier (IDFA)**: only if you allow it in the ATT dialog described below.

The App asks once, via the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) dialog, for permission to use the IDFA, either after you finish the tutorial or on your second or later launch, whichever comes first. If you do not allow it, the IDFA is not collected and is not sent to Meta; in that case, installs that come from ads are still counted through Apple's SKAdNetwork or similar means, in a way that does not identify you or your device. If you allow it, the information above, including the IDFA, is used by Meta to measure the effectiveness of our ads and optimize their delivery. You can change this setting at any time in your device's Settings app (Privacy & Security > Tracking).

No personally identifying information (such as your name or email address) and no game progress data are sent to Meta. See Meta's privacy policy at https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/.

## 5. Sharing With Third Parties

We never sell your information. Except for the service providers (subprocessors) and advertising partner described below, we do not share or otherwise disclose your information to third parties.

The App bundles the RevenueCat, Inc. ("RevenueCat") SDK to manage in-app purchase status and for sales analytics. Only purchase transaction information and an anonymous app user ID randomly generated by the SDK are sent to RevenueCat; no personally identifying information (such as your name or email address) and no game progress data are sent. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

The App bundles the PostHog, Inc. ("PostHog") SDK to help us improve the App. Anonymous usage data about in-app actions, such as opening screens, building courses and lifts, unlocking maps, and using the shop, is sent to PostHog along with basic information such as your device model, OS version, and app version. This data is associated only with an anonymous ID randomly generated by the SDK; it is not linked to any personally identifying information (such as your name or email address) and is not used for tracking or advertising. The data is processed on PostHog's servers in the EU region. See PostHog's privacy policy at https://posthog.com/privacy.

The App bundles the Meta SDK to measure the effectiveness of our ads and optimize their delivery. Only app events such as launches, basic device information, and, only if you allow it through ATT, the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) are sent to Meta (see Section 4, "Advertising and Tracking," for details). See Meta's privacy policy at https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/.

Other than as described above, the App does not bundle any third-party crash-reporting or other framework that transmits data externally.

## 6. In-App Purchases (StoreKit)

The App offers the following items as in-app purchases. All purchases and billing are processed entirely through Apple's StoreKit.

- Income Boost (consumable 6-hour In-App Purchase, product ID `jp.ne.alicemagic.yukiguni.boost.income2x.6h`)
- Offline Plus (consumable 6-hour In-App Purchase, product ID `jp.ne.alicemagic.yukiguni.boost.offlineplus.6h`)
- Time Warp 4h / 12h / 24h (consumable In-App Purchases, product IDs `jp.ne.alicemagic.yukiguni.warp.4h` / `jp.ne.alicemagic.yukiguni.warp.12h` / `jp.ne.alicemagic.yukiguni.warp.24h`)
- Cosmetic items, 12 in total: Groomer Paint ×4 (Crimson / Royal Blue / Forest Green / Sakura Pink) / Board ×4 (Fishtail / Retro Stripe / Thunderbolt / Sunset) / Wear ×4 (Festival Happi / Bear Suit / 80s One-Piece / Street Black) (non-consumable In-App Purchases, product IDs `jp.ne.alicemagic.yukiguni.cosme.*`)

Non-consumable items can be restored with the same Apple ID via "Restore Purchases" in the App. Consumable items take effect at the time of purchase and cannot be restored.

All purchase processing is handled entirely by Apple's App Store / StoreKit. We never receive or store payment information (such as credit card numbers).

## 7. Deleting Your Data

To remove the game progress stored on your device:

- Choose "Reset save data" from the App's menu (the effects of purchased permanent items are kept), or
- Delete (uninstall) the App from your device. Removing the App also removes the data stored in its storage area.

## 10. The App's Website

The App's introduction website (the "Site") uses PostHog (PostHog, Inc., EU region) to understand how the Site is viewed and to improve it. The Site sends PostHog only page views and page exits (including how far the page was scrolled), clicks on App Store links, the display language, and basic information such as browser type, screen size, and referring URL. PostHog measurement does not use cookies or browser local storage; it uses a temporary ID that is valid only while the page is open. IP addresses are anonymized by PostHog and are not stored. We do not collect personally identifying information such as your name or email address, and the data is not used for tracking or advertising.

The Site also uses Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC) to understand how the Site is accessed. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymous information such as page views and browser type. The data is managed under Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not use Google signals or ad personalization. You can opt out by disabling cookies in your browser or by using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on (https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
