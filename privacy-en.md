# Yukiguni Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

SEVEN DOT, LIMITED LIABILITY COMPANY ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the iOS application "Yukiguni" ("the App") handles.

## 1. Information We Collect

**We do not collect any information that personally identifies you, such as your name, email address, or location.** The App has no account registration or login.

Your game progress is stored only on your device and is never sent to our servers or to any third party (see Section 2, "Information Stored On Your Device"). However, to improve the App, we collect anonymous usage data that is not linked to you personally (see Section 5, "Sharing With Third Parties," for details).

The App includes the RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) SDK to manage in-app purchases; only purchase transaction information and an anonymous app user ID randomly generated by the SDK are sent to RevenueCat. The App also includes the PostHog (PostHog, Inc., EU region) SDK to understand how the App is used (see Section 5, "Sharing With Third Parties," for details). The App contains no advertising SDK.

## 2. Information Stored On Your Device

The App stores the following information only inside your iPhone / iPad (in the App's own storage area). None of this data is sent to our servers or to any third party.

- **Game progress**: the state of your slopes, courses, lifts, and facilities, your coins, construction progress, unlocked maps, and similar data.
- **Purchased items (cache)**: whether you own the permanent items is also stored on your device, so their effects stay active even when you launch the App offline.

Because game progress exists only on your device, the App has no feature to transfer your progress to another device.

## 3. Access to Device Features

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, photos, contacts, or any other device feature or personal data.

## 4. Advertising and Tracking

The App shows no ads. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and it does not collect the Advertising Identifier (IDFA).

If we advertise the App, we may use Apple's SKAdNetwork to anonymously measure the number of installs that come from ads. SKAdNetwork does not provide us or advertising partners with any information that identifies you or your device.

## 5. Sharing With Third Parties

We never sell your information. Except for the service provider (subprocessor) described below, we do not share or otherwise disclose your information to third parties.

The App bundles the RevenueCat, Inc. ("RevenueCat") SDK to manage in-app purchase status and for sales analytics. Only purchase transaction information and an anonymous app user ID randomly generated by the SDK are sent to RevenueCat; no personally identifying information (such as your name or email address) and no game progress data are sent. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

The App bundles the PostHog, Inc. ("PostHog") SDK to help us improve the App. Anonymous usage data about in-app actions, such as opening screens, building courses and lifts, unlocking maps, and using the shop, is sent to PostHog along with basic information such as your device model, OS version, and app version. This data is associated only with an anonymous ID randomly generated by the SDK; it is not linked to any personally identifying information (such as your name or email address) and is not used for tracking or advertising. The data is processed on PostHog's servers in the EU region. See PostHog's privacy policy at https://posthog.com/privacy.

Other than as described above, the App does not bundle any third-party advertising, crash-reporting, or other framework that transmits data externally.

## 6. In-App Purchases (StoreKit)

The App offers the following items as in-app purchases. All purchases and billing are processed entirely through Apple's StoreKit.

- Income Boost (consumable 6-hour In-App Purchase, product ID `jp.ne.alicemagic.yukiguni.boost.income2x.6h`)
- Offline Plus (consumable 6-hour In-App Purchase, product ID `jp.ne.alicemagic.yukiguni.boost.offlineplus.6h`)
- Time Warp 4h / 12h / 24h (consumable In-App Purchases, product IDs `jp.ne.alicemagic.yukiguni.warp.4h` / `jp.ne.alicemagic.yukiguni.warp.12h` / `jp.ne.alicemagic.yukiguni.warp.24h`)
- Cosmetic items, 12 in total: Groomer Paint ×4 (Crimson / Royal Blue / Forest Green / Sakura Pink) / Board ×4 (Fishtail / Retro Stripe / Thunderbolt / Sunset) / Wear ×4 (Festival Happi / Bear Suit / 80s One-Piece / Street Black) (non-consumable In-App Purchases, product IDs `jp.ne.alicemagic.yukiguni.cosme.*`)

Non-consumable items can be restored with the same Apple ID via "Restore Purchases" in the App. Consumable items take effect at the time of purchase and cannot be restored.

All purchase processing is handled entirely by Apple's App Store / StoreKit. We never receive or store payment information (such as credit card numbers).

## 7. Deleting Your Data

To remove the game progress stored on your device:

- Choose "Reset save data" from the App's menu (the effects of purchased permanent items are kept), or
- Delete (uninstall) the App from your device. Removing the App also removes the data stored in its storage area.
